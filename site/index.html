Hollow

Carve it · Light it · Don't trust what it casts

Carve a pumpkin with your pointer, then light the candle. A WGSL shader lights the room through whatever you cut, and throws your carving across the wall. Leave it lit long enough and the light shows a face you didn't carve.

What you can do

A knife, a candle, and something on the wall

Knife

Drag to cut. Close the shape and the piece pops out and tumbles onto the table. Leave it open for a slit of light.

Etch

Shave the skin instead of cutting through. Once the candle is lit, the thin flesh glows amber.

Stencils

Grin, Ghoul, and Mashrabiya (مشربية), after the carved wooden screens of old Beirut houses. The script carves each one star by star.

Candle

It catches like a struck match. Sweep past it fast and the flame gutters. Snuff it and the eyes on the wall stay a moment longer.

How it's built

Three text files and the Rive CLI

scene.rmlThe artboard markup: the words, the control panel, a view model and three state-machine layers (the title rises in, the tool pill slides with a hand-tuned overshoot, the candle button crossfades). Click listeners write the view model.
main.luauA scripted layout that owns the room. It handles pointer carving, stencil jobs, falling pieces, the candle, the gust and the ghost. Every frame it draws five offscreen canvases and hands them to the shader.
light.wgslOne GPU Canvas pass: moonlight, glow through the cuts, warmth in the rind, the flame seen through the holes, a ray march toward the flame, your carving projected into the haze, and the thing on the wall.

Every frame of the video is a headless rive --screenshot run that replays real pointer events (down, move, up) through the runtime. The same input drives the knife, the buttons and the sliders.
